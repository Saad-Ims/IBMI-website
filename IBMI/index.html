What is the FIRST Robotics Competition?

FRC is an international high school robotics competition. Student teams have a few weeks to design, build and program an industrial-size robot, then compete with it in alliances against other teams.

How it works

Each season, FIRST announces a new game. Every team receives the rules and a standard kit of parts, then builds a robot to play it. At events, three teams join together as an alliance, so success depends on cooperating with teams you have just met.

Who builds the robot

Students do. Mechanical designers model and machine the structure, electrical students wire the control system, programmers write the code for autonomous and driver-controlled play, and the business team raises funds and tells the team's story.

Why it matters

FRC gives students real engineering practice: teamwork, problem solving, project deadlines, budgeting and communication. It also opens doors to universities, scholarships and technical careers.

International competition shows what young Moroccan talent can do. Every match we play puts our country's students on a global stage and inspires others at home to try robotics.

Our departments

Four departments, one robot. Each one owns a part of the project and depends on the others.

Electrical

The electrical team is the robot's nervous system. They design the power distribution, wire the motors, sensors and radio, manage cables and connectors, and test every circuit so the robot behaves reliably in each match.
